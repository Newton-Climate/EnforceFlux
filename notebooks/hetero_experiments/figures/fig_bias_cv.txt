A scatter and line plot of signed total-flux retrieval error in percent against source heterogeneity amplitude CV, for open-path and point-sensor networks. Individual runs are open markers and the mean of the twelve runs at each CV is a filled marker joined by a line. A horizontal line marks zero error. Both geometries move from a small positive bias at CV 0.5 to a large negative bias at CV 2.0, where the open-path mean is minus 11.9 percent and the point-sensor mean is minus 12.5 percent, and every one of the twelve open-path runs underestimates the true flux.
