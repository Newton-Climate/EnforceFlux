Two panels comparing the two network types on 288 matched configurations that share patch size, patchiness amplitude, emission seed and instrument count. The left panel is a histogram of point-sensor absolute error minus open-path absolute error in percentage points; positive values mean the open path was closer to the truth. The distribution is centred right of zero with a median of +3.6 percentage points, and open path is closer in 182 of 288 pairs, which a sign test puts at p = 4e-6. The right panel gives the percentage of pairs favouring open path at each instrument count: 79 percent at n = 1, falling to about 57 to 60 percent for n = 2 to 4, so a single open path is where path integration helps most and added point sensors close much of the gap.
