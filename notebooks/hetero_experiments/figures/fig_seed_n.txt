Two panels against the number of instruments n from 1 to 4, over 576 inversions. The left panel shows mean absolute total-flux error. Open path is flat at about 11 to 11 percent for every n, so extra paths do not improve accuracy. Point sensors start much worse at 19 percent for a single sensor and improve to 11 percent at four, converging on the open-path value; four point sensors are needed to match one open path. The right panel shows the uncertainty the retrieval reports, as a percentage of the true flux. It falls from about 39 percent at n = 1 to 21 percent at n = 4 for both networks, tracking a plotted one-over-root-n reference curve. Reported uncertainty therefore shrinks with sampling effort while true accuracy does not, so a network can report growing confidence in an estimate that is no closer to the truth.
