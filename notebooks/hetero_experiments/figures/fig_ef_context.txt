A histogram of implied rice methane emission factors in kilograms per hectare per year across 82 Sacramento Valley grid cells of 0.1 degree, binned every 100 units from 0 to 2000. A shaded band marks the IPCC reference range of 300 to 600 and three vertical lines mark the EPA gridded inventory value of 742, the CARB value of 143, and the distribution median of 700. The distribution is broad and right-skewed and the two agency estimates differ by a factor of 5.2, with CARB below and EPA above the IPCC range.
