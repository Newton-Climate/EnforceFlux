A scatter plot of point-sensor absolute error against open-path absolute error in percent for all 36 matched configurations, with equal axes from 0 to 28 percent and a 1 to 1 reference line. Marker size encodes CV and marker shape encodes network size n. Points above the line, where open path does better, number 24 of 36. The largest markers, which are the CV 2.0 cases, sit farthest from the origin, and the cases where point sensors win cluster at low error and low CV near the origin.
