Two panels for point-sensor networks against emission contrast CV of 0.5, 1.0 and 2.0, using the 288 point-sensor inversions across eight source realizations. The left panel plots the signed total-flux error of every individual run as an open square with the mean and one standard deviation overlaid. The mean sits near zero at every contrast: +4.3, +2.3 and -0.5 percent, so heterogeneity introduces no consistent over- or under-estimate once source realizations are resampled. The right panel shows the run-to-run standard deviation growing steeply with contrast, from 10 to 17 to 26 percent, plotted against the much smaller absolute mean error on the same axes. Emission contrast therefore controls how far a single campaign can fall from the field mean, not the direction in which it falls.
