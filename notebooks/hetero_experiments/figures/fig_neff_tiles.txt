Three squares, each representing the same 1 km by 1 km field, tiled into independent source regions of side L. At L = 100 m the field holds about 100 regions; at 250 m about 16; at 500 m only 4. Shading of the tiles is random and illustrative, indicating that each region emits independently. The figure is conceptual: it shows that the number of effectively independent source regions falls as the square of patch scale, which is the quantity that limits how well any downwind sample can average over the field.
