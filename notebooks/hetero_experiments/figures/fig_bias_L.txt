A scatter and line plot of signed total-flux error in percent against source correlation length L of 100, 250 and 500 m, for open-path and point-sensor networks, with the twelve individual runs at each L shown as open markers behind the mean. Error becomes more negative as L grows, from minus 1.9 percent at 100 m to minus 8.2 percent at 500 m for open path, but the spread at each L is far larger than the trend, and the 100 m case spans only 2.5 source grid cells so it is only marginally resolved.
